# ReservKit SEO Page Inventory

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-03

This inventory separates live routes, navigation visibility, and worthwhile future pages. It is a content roadmap, not permission to publish thin pages or claim unsupported product capabilities.

## Navigation Positioning

- The primary navigation should lead with product evaluation: Features, How It Works, Pricing, and Docs.
- `Who it's for` is a secondary dropdown. Its links are representative high-priority audiences, not an exhaustive list of businesses ReservKit can support.
- The footer labels the current vertical links `Popular industries` for the same reason.
- Keep the public launch message focused on boat and watersports rentals while building broader search coverage around verified rental, tour, and activity workflows.

## Intentionally Not Listed Yet

- Multi-day or overnight rental pages: multi-day/overnight booking is not live.
- Marketplace, OTA, channel-manager, or distribution pages: those integrations are not live.
- Customer-selectable specific equipment pages: operators assign named units; customers do not select a unit during checkout.
- Inventory maintenance, barcode, repair, or warehouse pages: those workflows are not live.
- Multi-location enterprise pages: publish only after the product and support model are verified for that promise.
- FareHarbor, Peek Pro, Checkfront, Rezdy, Rezgo, Xola, and Booqable alternative/comparison pages: create only with a dated factual review, defensible comparison table, migration evidence, and legal review. The generic switching page is the current safe destination.
- Customer story/testimonial pages: publish only from real, attributable operator evidence. Do not create fake testimonials.

## Recommended Sequence

1. Build the two parent pages: equipment rental booking software and activity booking software.
2. Add ATV, UTV, bike, e-bike, snowmobile, paddleboard, canoe, fishing charter, and boat tour pages in small evidence-backed batches.
3. Create supporting blog articles and internal links for each cluster instead of relying on isolated landing pages.
4. Use Search Console and PostHog to measure impressions, qualified signup clicks, onboarding starts, and activated organizations by landing page.
5. Promote a page into `Who it's for` only when it is a priority acquisition audience; keep the dropdown intentionally short.
