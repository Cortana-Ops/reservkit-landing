# ReservKit Social Launch Plan

**Prepared:** 2026-09-10
**Profile setup:** LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram
**Paid campaign status:** Draft only; spending disabled

Social has two jobs before the public announcement: establish a credible
ReservKit presence and bring qualified operators into guided setup. It should
not manufacture customer proof or buy broad traffic before the activation
funnel works.

## Channel Order

1. **LinkedIn Company Page:** Primary B2B credibility and founder distribution.
2. **Facebook Page:** Required Meta business identity and useful for operator
   communities, Messenger, and later paid campaigns.
3. **Instagram Business:** Product visuals, operator workflow posts, and the
   shared Meta inbox.

Do not open TikTok, X, YouTube, or additional profiles until these three are
complete and actively maintained.

## Organic Content Sequence

Every post must use current production behavior and controlled demo images.
Do not imply that demo operators are customers.

### Post 1: What ReservKit Is

**Creative:** `public/social/reservkit-social-cover.png`
**Destination:** Homepage with `utm_content=what_reservkit_is`

Direct booking software should connect the whole operating day, not stop at a
checkout button.

ReservKit gives rental and experience operators one place for availability,
Stripe payments, customer records, waiver evidence, check-in, staff, and
reports.

Start on Free and build one working booking flow: https://reservkit.com

### Post 2: The First Bookable Flow

**Creative:** `public/product-public-booking-live.png`
**Destination:** Guided setup with `utm_content=first_bookable_flow`

The first ReservKit setup is intentionally concrete:

1. Create one real activity.
2. Add future availability.
3. Connect your Stripe account.
4. Open your public booking page.
5. Run and refund a controlled test booking.

Operators who want help can request guided setup:
https://reservkit.com/early-access

### Post 3: Payments Without Platform Confusion

**Creative:** `public/product-bookings-dashboard.png`
**Destination:** Pricing with `utm_content=payments_and_pricing`

ReservKit uses Stripe Connect so operators connect their own Stripe account.
Stripe processing is separate from ReservKit's plan-based booking fee, and the
public pricing page shows every self-serve tier.

See the exact pricing: https://reservkit.com/pricing

### Post 4: From Waiver To Check-In

**Creative:** `public/product-checkin-manifest.png`
**Destination:** Homepage with `utm_content=waiver_to_checkin`

A booking is not finished when payment clears. Operators still need waiver
status, attendee details, staff visibility, and a usable check-in workflow.

ReservKit keeps those records attached to the booking so the day-of team can
see what is ready and what still needs attention.

Explore the workflow: https://reservkit.com

### Post 5: Guided Setup Call

**Creative:** `public/product-activities.png`
**Destination:** Guided setup with `utm_content=guided_setup`

We are scheduling a small number of founder-guided ReservKit setups for rental
and experience operators.

The goal is one working booking flow, not a sales presentation. We will map an
activity, availability, Stripe Connect, the public booking page, and a
controlled booking test together.

Request a session: https://reservkit.com/early-access

Paid activation requires all of the following:

- At least 3 qualified operators have completed a first bookable flow.
- At least 5 qualified operators have entered onboarding.
- A fresh production Sentry review has no current-release launch blocker.
- The signup and guided-setup attribution path is verified.
- The owner explicitly approves the final audience, dates, and maximum spend.
- Privacy and consent behavior is reviewed before adding Meta Pixel or
  Conversions API tracking. Do not add Meta tracking merely to launch an ad.

Start with one campaign, one ad set, and two creatives. Do not create parallel
campaigns, automatic budget expansion, or an open-ended daily budget. Pause if
qualified guided-setup requests do not appear after the initial $70 test.
